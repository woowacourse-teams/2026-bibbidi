low=Greetings and simple questions answered by explanation alone, with no judgment needed, such as what a term or procedure means. Example: 스드메 상담은 뭘 하는 건가요?
medium=Recommendations and advice that reflect one or two conditions: progress, region or budget. Also output requirements such as count, format or language, empathy for anxiety or family conflict, plain follow-ups to the previous answer, and refusal of inappropriate or out-of-scope requests. Example: 웨딩홀 계약은 끝났어요. 다음 준비 항목 두 개를 추천해 주세요.
high=Requests that need real reasoning: judging a state that changed or contradicts earlier statements, filtering out items already done or registered before deciding the next step, summarizing the user's overall preparation status, and prioritizing among conflicting conditions such as budget, schedule, region and family opinions. Example: 아까는 안 했는데 그 사이에 웨딩홀 투어를 다녀왔어요. 이제 뭐부터 할까요?
